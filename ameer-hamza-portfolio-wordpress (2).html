Hair Transplant Clinic: Lowering the Barrier to Booking

Industry: Healthcare / cosmetic procedure clinic · US

Challenge

High-ticket hair transplant services naturally require more consideration, making it difficult to generate affordable and qualified prospects.

Strategy

Shifted to video-based creatives, highlighted the clinic experience and treatment process, introduced a free consultation offer, and tested messaging to attract higher-intent prospects.

Result

Generated more consultation-focused inquiries and helped create opportunities for prospects to move further into the booking process.

Case Study 02 · US Healthcare Clinic

US Healthcare Clinic: Lead Generation Performance

Challenge

The clinic needed a consistent flow of leads while maintaining an efficient cost per lead.

Strategy

Refined audience targeting, tested different ad creatives and messaging angles, monitored CPL and lead volume, and focused on better-performing ads.
